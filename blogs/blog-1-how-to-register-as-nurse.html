How to Register as a Nurse with Interim Health: Step-by-Step Guide

Learn how to register as a nurse with South Africa's leading healthcare staffing agency and gain access to premium locum and permanent nursing jobs nationwide.

If you're a qualified nurse in South Africa looking for locum nurse jobs or permanent placements, registering with a reputable nursing recruitment agency is one of the best career moves you can make. With over 21 years of experience in healthcare staffing, Interim Health has helped thousands of nurses find rewarding positions across the country.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll walk you through exactly how to register as a nurse with Interim Health, what documents you'll need, and what happens after you submit your application. Whether you're a registered nurse, enrolled nurse, or caregiver, this step-by-step process will get you started on your next career opportunity.

Why Register with a Nursing Recruitment Agency?

Before we dive into the registration process, it's important to understand why working with a nursing recruitment agency South Africa trusts can transform your career. Here are the key benefits:

Access to Exclusive Nursing Jobs South Africa

Many of the best nursing jobs South Africa has to offer are never advertised publicly. Healthcare facilities partner directly with recruitment agencies like Interim Health to fill positions quickly and efficiently. By registering with us, you get access to:

  • Locum nursing jobs in Gauteng, Western Cape, and nationwide
  • Permanent nursing positions at top hospitals and clinics
  • Specialized roles in critical care, theatre, paediatrics, and more
  • Competitive rates and flexible scheduling options

SANC Verification Made Simple

One of the biggest challenges nurses face when applying for jobs is the SANC verification process. Every healthcare facility in South Africa requires proof of current SANC registration, and navigating this bureaucracy can be time-consuming. At Interim Health, we handle the entire SANC verification process for you, ensuring your credentials are validated quickly and correctly.

Did you know? All nurses placed through Interim Health undergo mandatory SANC verification. This compliance requirement gives healthcare facilities complete peace of mind and ensures you're matched with legitimate employers.

Step-by-Step Registration Process

Registering as a nurse with Interim Health is straightforward and can be completed in three simple steps. The entire process takes approximately 15-20 minutes, and our team will guide you through each stage.

Step 1: Personal Information

The first step is to provide your basic personal details. You'll need to have your ID number or passport ready. This information helps us create your profile and verify your identity in compliance with POPIA regulations.

You'll also provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so our recruitment team can reach you about relevant locum nurse jobs and permanent placements. Make sure to include your physical address as well, as some positions may require you to be within a specific geographic area.

Step 2: Professional Qualifications

In this step, you'll detail your professional background. This is where you'll enter your SANC registration number, which is crucial for verification. Our system will cross-reference this with the SANC database to confirm your current registration status.

You'll also select your professional qualification from our comprehensive list, which includes:

  • • Registered Nurse (Diploma or Degree)
  • • Enrolled Nurse
  • • Enrolled Nursing Auxiliary
  • • Critical Care Nurse
  • • Midwife, Psychiatric Nurse, Community Health Nurse
  • • Caregiver / Home Carer

Be sure to indicate your years of experience and whether you're currently practicing. This helps us match you with the most suitable nursing jobs South Africa has available.

You'll also select your specializations and skills from our comprehensive list, including general nursing, critical care/ICU, paediatrics, maternity/midwifery, mental health/psychiatry, geriatric care, theatre/surgical, and primary health care. This ensures we can match you with positions that align with your expertise.

Step 3: Documents and Finalize

The final step involves uploading your documents and indicating your work preferences. You'll need to upload your CV or resume in PDF, DOC, or DOCX format. Having a well-prepared nursing CV is essential for securing the best positions.

You'll also indicate your preferred work type:

  • ✓ Locum/Temporary: Flexible shifts, great pay, diverse experiences
  • ✓ Permanent: Stable career with benefits and growth opportunities
  • ✓ Both: Maximum flexibility to choose what suits your lifestyle

Finally, you'll indicate your availability and confirm that you have your SANC certificate, ID/passport copy, and qualification certificates ready to provide. You'll also need to provide POPIA consent, which allows us to process your personal information for recruitment purposes in compliance with South African data protection laws.

What Happens After You Register?

Once you've submitted your registration application, our recruitment team will review your details within 2-3 business days. Here's what you can expect:

SANC Verification and Credentialing

Our team will verify your SANC registration against the official SANC database. This mandatory verification ensures that you're qualified to practice in South Africa and that your registration is current. We'll also review your qualifications and experience to build a comprehensive profile.

Profile Activation and Job Matching

Once verified, your profile becomes active in our database. Our sophisticated matching system will then identify locum nurse jobs and permanent positions that align with your skills, experience, and preferences. We work with hospitals, clinics, and healthcare facilities across South Africa, from major metropolitan areas to rural communities.

Direct Communication and Placement

When a suitable opportunity arises, our recruitment consultants will contact you directly with the details. We'll discuss the position, location, rate, and schedule to ensure it's the right fit. Once you accept an offer, we handle all the administrative details, including contracts and compliance documentation, so you can focus on what you do best—providing excellent patient care.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the nurse registration process take?

The registration process typically takes 2-3 business days once all required documents are submitted. This includes SANC verification and credentialing review. We strive to make the process as efficient as possible so you can start receiving job opportunities quickly.

What documents do I need to register as a nurse?

You'll need your SANC registration certificate, ID/passport copy, qualification certificates, CV/resume, and proof of address. Having these documents ready before you start the registration process will speed things up significantly.

Is SANC verification mandatory for registration?

Yes, SANC verification is mandatory for all nurses registered with Interim Health. We verify every candidate against the SANC database to ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and to maintain the trust of our healthcare facility partners. This verification process is part of what makes Interim Health a trusted nursing recruitment agency South Africa relies on.

Can I register as both a locum and permanent nurse?

Absolutely! During registration, you can indicate your preference for locum, permanent, or both. This gives you the flexibility to choose the type of work that best suits your lifestyle and career goals. Many nurses start with locum positions to gain experience before transitioning to permanent roles.

Are there any fees to register with Interim Health?

No, registration with Interim Health is completely free for nurses and healthcare professionals. We only earn a fee when we successfully place you in a position. There are no hidden costs or upfront payments required to join our network.
